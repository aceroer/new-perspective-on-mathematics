Submission channel

Share a new perspective, question, or route.

NPM welcomes mathematical work that opens a direction: a complete paper, a route, a conceptual framework, a computational report, a formalized artifact, a conjecture, or a public branch workflow.

Required Materials

  • Problem statement and target claim.
  • Status label: exploration, route, audit, formalized, verified-sample, release, or paper-draft.
  • Route card or research note describing definitions, expected lemmas, and known risks.
  • Claim boundary separating proof, formalization, computation, and interpretation.
  • Links to code, Lean files, scripts, logs, or release artifacts when available.

How To Submit

  1. Open an issue in the GitHub repository.
  2. Use the title format: Submission: short title.
  3. Attach or link the route card and audit report.
  4. State whether the work is intended as a paper, research note, exploratory framework, or branch workflow.
