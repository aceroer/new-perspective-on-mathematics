About

Mathematics is an evolving exploration.

New Perspective on Mathematics (NPM) is an open research community dedicated to exploring mathematics through new perspectives, new questions, and new connections.

We believe that mathematics is not a completed structure, but an evolving exploration. Throughout history, the greatest advances have often emerged not from refining existing maps, but from discovering entirely new territories.

NPM was founded in the belief that many important discoveries may still lie beyond the boundaries of current frameworks. Our goal is not to defend any particular school of thought, nor to promote a predefined answer. Instead, we seek to create an open space where new ideas can be explored, challenged, refined, and shared.

Our Philosophy

We keep the map open.

  • Questions can be more important than answers.
  • Perspectives can be more valuable than conclusions.
  • Exploration itself deserves to be recorded.
  • Mathematics remains an unfinished frontier.
  • New mathematical languages may reveal new realities.
  • Cross-disciplinary exploration is often the birthplace of innovation.
