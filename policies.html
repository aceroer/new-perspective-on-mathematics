Editorial policy

Open exploration, clear boundaries.

NPM welcomes unconventional ideas when they are presented with clarity, sincerity, and intellectual honesty. Exploration is welcome; unclear claims and hidden assumptions are not.

Claim Labels

Every article should label its claims as exploration, route claim, formal theorem, verified sample, interpretive claim, or release claim.

Constructive Dialogue

Disagreement is welcome. Review should clarify definitions, assumptions, evidence, and possible next questions.

Formal Artifacts

Lean or other proof-assistant files are welcome when present. Failed formalization attempts may be valuable when the proof state is recorded clearly.

Computation

Computational evidence must state commands, ranges, identities checked, failures, and version information.

AI Disclosure

AI collaboration is encouraged. Authors only need to state which model or system was used when AI-assisted work is part of the submission. NPM welcomes experiments in human-AI collaboration as a legitimate research method.

Beyond Current Frameworks

NPM encourages cross-disciplinary theories that move beyond current frameworks. A bridge theory does not need to be fully completed at submission if its central claims are stated clearly and its proposed mechanisms are verifiable, reproducible, or open to independent testing.
