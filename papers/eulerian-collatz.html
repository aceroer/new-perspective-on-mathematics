An Eulerian Note on the Accelerated Collatz Map

Wangyue

New Perspective on Mathematics · Issue 0
4 June 2026 · Manuscript version 0.2

Abstract

We discuss the accelerated odd Collatz map

T(n) = (3n + 1) / 2v₂(3n + 1) (n odd).

The note keeps together three elementary structures which are often treated separately: finite products along odd orbits, circular phases obtained from fractional logarithms, and the residue obstruction modulo 3. The purpose is to present an Eulerian unified observation: the accelerated map may be read at once as an exact multiplicative balance, a motion on the unit circle, and a one-way passage through residue classes. In this language a non-principal obstruction, if visible in the representation, would have to preserve a finite product balance, a non-principal circular mode, and compatibility with the many-to-one structure of the map on the two non-zero residue classes modulo 3. The result is not presented as a proof of the Collatz conjecture; it is a compact classical formulation of what a self-sustaining non-principal branch would have to carry.
